THERAPY · PHILOSOPHY

Returning home
to ourselves.

THERAPY is more than an album — it is storytelling in music. It was born from lived experience, from emotions and questions that stayed for years until they gradually found their voice.

We look for acceptance in family, relationships, nature, therapy, success or something greater than ourselves. But after loss, disappointment and pain, we begin to understand that true love, freedom and inner support are not found outside. They are discovered within.
